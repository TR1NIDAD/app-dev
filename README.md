# My Favorite Series

## The Mentalist
**The Mentalist** is a crime drama about Patrick Jane, a former fake psychic who now uses his sharp observation skills to help solve murder cases.

## My Favorite Character
My favorite character is **Patrick Jane**.

- He is very *charismatic* and can talk his way through almost any situation.
- His story is well built because his main goal throughout the series is to catch Red John.
- His past gives every case he works on a deeper meaning.

## Why I Love the Red John Storyline
I enjoy the episodes that focus on Red John the most because they connect to the main goal of the whole series.

## Favorite Episode
1. Strawberries and Cream (Season 3 finale)
2. Other episodes that focus on Red John

> In the Season 3 finale, Jane kills the man he believes is Red John, but it turns out he was not the real one.
